**### Programs List ###**

1.  **Shopping Bill** --- Calculates the total cost of notebooks and pens.
2.  **Monthly Electricity Bill** --- Calculates an electricity bill using units consumed and price per unit.
3.  **Student Marks Calculator** --- Calculates total marks, average and percentage for five subjects.
4.  **Salary Calculator** --- Calculates gross salary from basic salary and allowances.
5.  **Petrol Expense Calculator** --- Calculates petrol consumed and total fuel cost.
6.  **Restaurant Bill Sharing** --- Divides a restaurant bill equally among friends.
7.  **Age Calculator** --- Converts age in years into approximate months, weeks, and days.
8.  **Water Tank Capacity** --- Calculates tank volume in cubic metres and capacity in litres.
9.  **Simple Profit Calculator** --- Calculates total purchase cost, sales revenue, and profit or loss.
10. **Time Conversion** --- Converts total seconds into hours, minutes and remaining seconds.
